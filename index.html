ABOUT THE GAME

Every round of Rank Risk deals you six answer cards and one goal: put them in the right order. Slowest to fastest animal. Mildest to hottest pepper. Oldest to newest invention. Smallest to largest planet.

Before each question you decide how many Rank Points (RP) to risk. Play it safe, aim higher for a bigger payout, or go Max Risk and put everything on one answer.

When your order is wrong, the game only tells you how many cards are in the right position, not which ones. Rethink, swap, and try again before your four attempts run out. Win to grow your RP, build streaks for bonus payouts, and climb from Rookie to Legend.

FEATURES

  • 120 ranking puzzles across 12 categories, each showing the real values and a fun fact when you solve it
  • Risk your RP before every question, including all-in Max Risk moments

  • Win streaks with bonus payouts, 8 ranks from Rookie to Legend
  • 27 Steam achievements and global leaderboards
  • Short, replayable runs that fit a coffee break or a long stream

HOW IT PLAYS

1. RANK THE CARDS

Drag six cards into the slots. Here: animals from slowest to fastest.

2. READ THE FEEDBACK

Wrong? You learn how many cards are right. Power-ups like Arrows point the way.

3. RISK AND WIN

Bet RP before you answer, use power-ups like Peek, and climb the ranks.
