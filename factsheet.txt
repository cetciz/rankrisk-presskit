SHORT DESCRIPTION
Rank six answer cards in the correct order, choose how much RP to risk, and use limited
attempts to solve each puzzle. Build streaks, chase bigger payouts, and climb the
leaderboards in this fast-paced trivia ranking game.

ABOUT
Every round deals you six answer cards and one goal: put them in the right order. Slowest
to fastest animal. Mildest to hottest pepper. Oldest to newest invention. Smallest to
largest planet. Before each question you decide how many Rank Points (RP) to risk, up to
an all-in Max Risk. When your order is wrong, the game only tells you how many cards are
in the right position, not which ones. Rethink and try again before your four attempts run
out. Win to grow your RP, build streaks for bonus payouts, and climb from Rookie to Legend.
